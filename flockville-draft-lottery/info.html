HOW IT WORKS

Step-by-step

Already drafted last year? In Past Lotteries → Enter a Past Draft, transcribe your screenshot's actual season year and original teams in pick order, with optional pick owners. Choose complete order or partial top-three only; click Save / Confirm Past Draft and confirm. Missing results cannot be recovered automatically, and no old lottery is rerun.

Select the next season year, review automatically derived protection flags from exactly the preceding season, then draw and Export History for a backup. Protection follows the original entrant, not the traded-pick owner. If that year is missing, explicitly enable and review manual checkbox flags; both rule toggles work independently. Impossible rule combinations stop rather than silently relax protections.

Use Edit Selected Year to correct history with confirmation. Older finalized orders from full-state backups can be loaded with Import History and explicitly assigned years; no season is guessed. History views never replace live results.

  1. Add non-playoff teams in order from worst record to best record.
  2. Confirm pick owners if a team's first-round pick was traded.
  3. Review settings (lottery pick count, protections, cooldown rules, optional deterministic seed).
  4. Set the Season year and run the lottery draw to generate results.
  5. Review the final order and the required trades that put every pick in lottery order.
  6. The lottery is saved automatically to Past Lotteries under its year (re-running a saved year asks before replacing it).
  7. Optionally finalize the live season to archive outcomes and advance the season year. Saved history already supplies next year's protection.

QUICK START

Run it in under a minute

  1. Click Load 8-Team Demo for instant sample teams, or add teams manually.
  2. Keep default rules unless your league changed them.
  3. Click Start Lottery.
  4. Copy results with Copy Lottery Announcement.

WHY IT'S FAIR

Fairness and auditability

  • Weighted odds are transparent. The odds panel shows every team, ball count, and exact chance used by the draw.
  • Anti-tanking protections are configurable. Bottom-four protection, Top-3 cooldown, and no consecutive #1 reduce repeat abuse when enabled.
  • Tie-breaks are deterministic. Equal records are resolved in order: Strength of Schedule (Madden) → Head-to-Head (if available) → seeded deterministic coin flip.
  • Seeded mode is auditable. Enable deterministic seed to replay the same setup and verify identical results.

TRADED PICKS

How pick ownership works

The lottery entry belongs to the original team based on standings, but the final pick is awarded to the current owner. In the app, results display this as Owner (via Original Team).

  1. Set each team's Pick Owner in the main table.
  2. Run the draw as normal.
  3. If owner differs from team, the result card and announcement include the ownership note automatically.
  4. Use Generate Trades from Results to add every required pick swap to the trade log.

TRADES

How required trades are generated

Before the lottery, Pick #N belongs to whoever owns the Nth-worst team's pick (Madden's default order). After the draw, the app works out the pick swaps that move every pick to the team the lottery assigned it to.

  • Every move is covered. Trades are created whenever a pick needs to move, not only when a pick was traded earlier.
  • Current owners trade. If Baltimore owns the Bears' pick, Baltimore makes the swap.
  • Ordered swaps. With unique owners each cycle of length k needs k − 1 swaps. With duplicate owners a global minimum is not guaranteed. Invalid input displays an error, not “no trades needed”.
  • Clear wording. Each trade reads "Team A sends Pick #X to Team B for Pick #Y". Do them in the order listed.

HISTORY

Past lotteries

  • Each completed lottery is saved in your browser under its season year: date run, final order, odds used, and required trades.
  • Pick a year in Past Lotteries to view it, or click Delete This Year to remove one saved year.
  • Export History downloads all saved years as JSON. Import History merges a file back in and asks before replacing years you already have.
  • Reset does not delete Past Lotteries.
  • Storage is browser-local, not server-synced. Export files are backups; clearing browser data or changing devices can lose unexported records.
  • Manually entered records are labeled, unknown draw metadata stays unknown, and partial top-three records never generate trades.

EXAMPLE

Practical examples

Suppose Team A finishes with the second-worst record, but Team B owns Team A's first-round pick. If Team A's entry wins Pick #1 in the draw, Team B receives Pick #1 because ownership follows the traded pick.

Expected outcome in results: Pick #1 — Team B (via Team A).

Tie-break example: if two teams have the same record, the app first checks Madden SoS, then head-to-head data (if provided), then a seeded deterministic coin flip so repeated runs with the same setup are reproducible.

FAQ

Common questions

  • Why didn't a bottom-four team drop below Pick #8? Bottom-four protection prevents that when enabled.
  • Why couldn't last year's Top 3 land in the Top 3 again? Top-3 cooldown blocks repeat Top-3 outcomes when enabled.
  • What does deterministic seed do? It makes the draw repeatable for audit purposes using the same inputs and seed text.
  • What if tie-break data is missing? The app keeps existing ordering behavior and only applies SoS/H2H when those values are available.
  • Do I need a backend? No. The app runs fully in your browser and saves state locally.
