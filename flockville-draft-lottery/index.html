HOW IT WORKS

The lottery, explained

Who is eligible

Every non-playoff team enters, listed from worst record to best. Ties are broken by Strength of Schedule, then head-to-head (if available), then a seeded coin flip.

The odds

The four worst teams get 3 balls, the next four get 2, and everyone else gets 1. A team's chance on each draw is its balls divided by the balls still in the machine.

How the draw works

Picks #1 through #8 (configurable) are drawn one at a time. A drawn team is removed. Teams not drawn fill the remaining picks in reverse record order.

  • Top-3 cooldown: last year's Top 3 can't land in the Top 3.
  • No consecutive #1: last year's #1 can't win #1 again.
  • Bottom-four protection: the four worst teams can't fall below Pick #8.

How trades are determined

Before the lottery, Pick #N belongs to the Nth-worst team's pick owner. The app lists swaps between current owners to reach the final ownership order; execute them in order. Duplicate owners do not carry a global minimum-swap guarantee.

Example: if the 3rd-worst team wins #1, "Team C sends Pick #3 to Team A for Pick #1".

Review and edit the staged trades before archiving this season and advancing the year. Confirm saves the reviewed trade list; it does not execute trades in Madden. Cancel discards staged edits and returns without finalizing.

Override Protection Flags

Manual overrides disable auto-sync for this flag only. Choose original lottery teams for the selected flag; the other flag's source is unchanged. Saving applies to this season; cancel makes no change. Clear the override to resume history-based sourcing.
